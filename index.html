The Chroma Process

How we get your
colours exactly right.

You see exactly how your space will look before we open a single tin of paint. Every colour confirmed. Every shade approved in writing. No surprises on the day.

01Intake & Site Visit
02Photo Collection
03Colour Strategy
04Colour Board Presentation
05Client Approval
06Precision Mixing & Execution
07Completion & Warranty
01

Intake & Site Visit

You receive a short intake form via WhatsApp. We ask about room function, lighting, existing furniture tones, style preferences, and colours to avoid. Then we schedule a free site visit within 48 hours. We never quote blindly over the phone.

Free site visit included
02

Photo Collection

We collect 4 to 6 photos of your space from key angles. Corners, windows, and main furniture. These give us everything we need to understand how light moves through your home and how colour will perform in it.

Via WhatsApp
03

Colour Strategy

Our colour consultants analyse your space in full. Natural light quality, room proportions, existing furniture tones, and your stated preferences. We develop three distinct palette options, each with a main wall colour, ceiling finish, trim, and optional accent, all specified to exact Dulux or Berger paint codes.

Expert colour analysis
04

Colour Board Presentation

We build a branded Chroma Colour Board PDF with all three palette options. Each option shows colour swatches, the palette name, the rationale, and exact paint codes. We also apply each palette to photos of your actual room so you see how it reads in your specific light before making any decision.

Branded Colour Board PDF
05

Client Approval

You choose your palette, or request a hybrid. Adjustments are made. Once you are satisfied, you confirm in writing. That confirmation is the point of no return. Not a single tin of paint is purchased until we have it. This protects you and protects us.

Written approval before any purchase
06

Precision Mixing & Execution

Your approved colour codes go to a Dulux or Berger store tinting machine. The exact colour is mixed from your specification. On Day 1 we apply a test panel on your actual wall and confirm it in both morning and evening light before the full job begins.

Tinting machine precision
07

Completion & Warranty

Before we call you for the final walkthrough, we inspect every surface ourselves. Every edge, every ceiling line, every fitting. Anything that is not right gets fixed before you sign off. Every Chroma job carries a 6-month touch-up warranty on workmanship, standard.

6-month warranty included
